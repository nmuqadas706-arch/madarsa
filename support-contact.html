Transparent Donation Process

In keeping with strict institutional transparency and Shariah compliance, all charitable funds (Zakat, Sadaqah, and General Atiyyat) are handled directly through authorized institutional channels under the oversight of Mohtamim Hazrat Maulana Abidullah and our governing trust.

We do not publish unverified online third-party payment gateways. Donors wishing to contribute via official bank transfer, provide in-kind nutritional supplies, or visit the madrasa premises in person are invited to submit the inquiry form below or visit the administration office in Karachi.
