Our Foundations

An Authentic Sanctuary for Classical Islamic Education

Rooted in a historic heritage dating back to 1954 and revitalized under revered scholarly guidance, Mahd al-Irshad al-Islami offers a serene, spiritually enriching environment where young minds internalize the Holy Quran with precise Tajweed, humility, and moral rectitude.

Hifz al-Quran & Tajweed

Comprehensive memorization of the Holy Quran taught under the close personal mentorship of experienced Ustadhs, focusing on exact pronunciation, tonal precision, and retention.

Character & Moral Tarbiyah

Education at our madrasa transcends academic recitation. We instill the prophetic values of honesty, respect, self-discipline, compassion, and exemplary social etiquette.

Full Boarding & Orphan Care

Providing complete lodging, nourishing daily meals, healthcare, and educational provisions at zero financial burden to resident students, including 18 orphan scholars.

Scholarly Legacy

Built on Trust, Sincerity, and Community Support

The madrasa’s foundations were laid by the blessed hands of senior Pakistani Islamic scholars, including Qutb al-Aqtab Hazrat Maulana Saleemullah Khan (may Allah have mercy on him). Initiated by the late Hazrat Maulana Inayatullah and now steered by Hazrat Maulana Abidullah, the institution stands as an enduring beacon of traditional scholarship in Karachi.

Occupying an officially designated plot in the Lines Area of Karachi, our campus unites daily congregational worship at Jamia Masjid Muhajir Makki with continuous day and boarding academic sessions for hundreds of aspiring students.
